Risk signal
VILT completion declining 6% annually since 2022 — structural disengagement risk
Across Audit and Tax, virtual instructor-led training completion has dropped from 81% to 63% in 3 years. Forecast shows further decline to 48% by 2027 without intervention. Recommend blended redesign with async pre-work.
Opportunity
Consulting Band C employees have 2.4× higher self-directed learning hours — scalable model exists
Data shows this cohort completes 40% of learning outside assigned programmes. Replicating this behaviour across Tax and Audit could add 18 additional learning hours per employee annually without L&D investment.
Watch signal
Residential learning ROI declining as cohort sizes shrink post-pandemic
Residential programmes now average 18 attendees vs 34 in 2019. Cost-per-learner has increased 87%. Predictive model suggests this modality needs fundamental redesign or consolidation by 2026.
